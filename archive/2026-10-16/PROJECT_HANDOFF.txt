4. CONTROLLED NATIVE HAULSIM ROAD-RESISTANCE EXPERIMENT (OCTOBER 15)

Research question: with one truck, payload and route held fixed, how does

Three native HAULSIM 3.8 build 585 experiments were executed and reconciled:
RR02 = 2%, RR03 = 3%, RR04 = 4% rolling resistance. One 120-minute replication
per level. Route A, one truck, 45 t payload, 391 kW assumed wheel-traction

Loading service 4 min including loader spotting; unloading 1 min plus separate
dump spotting. Traction coefficient 0.6 and ground-structure class 0 stay fixed.
Battery energy, charging and regeneration are disabled in these cases.

5. FORMULAS AND ANALYTICAL WORK

Standard traction balance:
F = m g [sin(theta) + Cr cos(theta)], theta = atan(local grade).
P_wheel = F v; estimated P_battery = P_wheel / eta_drive + P_aux.
Cr is a fraction, not a percentage number. A common wheel ceiling of 391 kW
comes from (480 - 20) x 0.85 under assumed 20 kW auxiliaries and 85% efficiency.
These equations are established mechanics, not a new scientific contribution.

The October 15 paper calculations use loaded mass 93,148 kg and a local 16%
ramp. At 10 km/h, estimated terminal demand for 2/3/4% resistance is about
550.8/580.3/609.7 kW, above the cited 480 kW continuous battery limit. Under the
assumed conversion, power-limited speeds are 8.67/8.21/7.80 km/h. These are
analytical segment calculations, not full-cycle native speed predictions.
Loaded gravitational energy over 135 m is 34.2668205 kWh.

Standard lumped thermal balance:
C_th dTb/dt = Q_battery(t) - UA(Tb - T_air).
For constant Q and air: Tb(t) = T_air + Q/UA +
[Tb(0) - T_air - Q/UA] exp[-UA t/C_th].
The illustrative bulk model excludes active refrigeration, local hotspots and
runaway reaction heat. Motor/drivetrain losses are not automatically battery heat.

October 15 manuscript illustration: C=4 MJ/K, Q=5 kW, UA=400 or 200 W/K;
starting at ambient 25 or 35°C. Four-hour outputs remain transient: tau values
10,000/20,000 s reach only 76.3%/51.3% of asymptotic temperature rise.

October 16 one-feature illustration: all cases start at the SAME 25°C, with
C=4 MJ/K, Q=5 kW and UA=400 W/K fixed. Changing ONLY assumed air temperature
25/35/40°C gives 120-minute bulk temperatures 31.416/36.548/39.114°C.
The duration matches RR03 but the constant heat is not taken from HAULSIM.
This is a checked equation demonstration, not calibrated battery prediction.
For fixed coefficients, dTb/dT_air = 1-exp(-UA t/C), so that trend is built in.

BEV_Temperature_Feature_2026-10-16/00_READ_FIRST.txt, temperature_cases.csv.

6. A123 LABORATORY THERMAL MODEL AND EXTERNAL HAULSIM COUPLING

Downloaded real A123 26650 m1b 2.5 Ah LFP cell data, DOI 10.17632/p8kf893yv3.1,
CC BY 4.0; author Aloisio Kawakita de Souza. Pinned GitHub source commit and
hashes are recorded. Current, surface temperature and surrounding-air
temperature are measured. These cells are not identified as Z50 cells.

Reduced formula: dTs/dt = a I_cell^2 - b(Ts - T_air).
Fitted only on A002_PeriodicPulseData.mat, then frozen:
a = 4.344635339312832e-5 K/(A² s), b = 0.002655462788692412 s^-1;
tau = 376.582 s. Only a=R_eff/C_eff and b=UA_eff/C_eff are identified.

Held-out same-cell records:
A002_UDDS_P25: RMSE 0.109021°C, versus air-following baseline 0.604398°C.
A002_UDDS_P35: RMSE 0.117148°C, versus air-following baseline 0.804507°C.
The baseline tracks measured air plus the initial sensor/air offset; this is
NOT the constant-temperature comparator in the later 228 Ah experiment.
Measured mean air is 26.12/36.72°C, not exactly the nominal filename setpoints.

The native RR03 schedule supplies 29 event states covering exactly 7,200 s.
External event-average load estimate:
P_batt,k = E_positive_wheel,k / (event_hours x 0.85) + 20 kW.
I_equivalent_cell,k = Q_cell_Ah x P_batt,k_kW / E_nominal_pack_kWh.
The latter is an equivalent-C-rate surrogate, not a measured cell/pack current
or a reconstruction of the actual series/parallel pack topology.

The wheel-energy numeric scale is interpreted as kWh. Four loaded-leg checks

export-unit metadata confirmation remains outstanding. Native battery/SOC is
disabled. The external estimate totals 331.86 kWh over two hours under assumed

validated endurance forecast. Event averaging loses peaks, and square of the
average current generally understates mean squared current.

Using A123 laboratory cooling with initial temperature fixed at 25°C and air

real-truck heating effect. The 40°C case is extrapolation. No pack bulk/core

9. LATEST EXPERIMENT — 228 Ah FORMULA VERSUS CONSTANT TEMPERATURE

Protocol fixed before fitting (after earlier descriptive data inspection).
Selected seven of 55 HPPC records: all six cells at 0D plus B1 at 7D.
573,292 observations, 159.073 total record-hours. These are six physical cells;
time samples and three temperature channels are not independent replicates.

Model: dT/dt = a I² - b(T - 25), a>=0, b>=0; coefficients refitted to these
228 Ah records, NOT copied from A123 or assumed Z50 values.
Primary baseline: T(t)=measured T(0). Additional comparator: cooling-only,
a=0 with b fitted on training records. Nominal 25°C ambient is assumed.

Primary evaluation: six-fold leave-one-cell-out; fit five whole initial cell
records, test the sixth. Fit each channel label separately; report all three.
Secondary chronological evaluation: calibrate B1 0D, test B1 7D separately.
No random row split, test-mean correction, retuning on held-out measurements
or post-initialization temperature feedback. Cross-cell label equivalence is
unconfirmed because probe positions are not mapped.

Exact integration with left-held current and true sample intervals. Profile b
over zero and 1e-7..1 s^-1, solve nonnegative a analytically, refine local minima.
Loss weights each training record equally; within-record errors are time-
weighted. Coefficients frozen before test summaries. Aggregate RMSE is square
root of equal-weight mean channel-record MSE, not average RMSE.
