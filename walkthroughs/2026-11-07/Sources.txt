1. NINE HAULAGE CASES AND ACCOUNTING
reproducibility/data/workspace/APCOM_Study_2026-10-22/results/operations_comparison.csv
reproducibility/data/workspace/APCOM_Study_2026-10-22/results/operations_audit.json
reproducibility/src/bev_research/haulage.py

Reserve is applied outside HAULSIM. It is 60% of nominal energy, representing
80% initial energy to 20% reserve. The crossing is interpolated within an event
at its recorded average power. Deliveries count only when unloading finishes.
The model includes one truck and one replication per case; the planned central
repeat was not executed. Geometry and road conditions contain assumptions.

2. TEMPERATURE MODEL
reproducibility/src/bev_research/thermal_model.py
reproducibility/docs/MODEL_AND_LIMITS.md
reproducibility/data/workspace/APCOM_Study_2026-10-22/results/thermal_audit.json

Equivalent current = 2.5 Ah * event-average kW / nominal kWh.
This maps normalized power onto a reference cell. It does not identify actual
pack current or the current in an actual Z50 cell. The fitted thermal model
describes an A123-equivalent cell surface under stated assumptions. Same-cell
comparison residuals are not uncertainty bounds for a full truck battery.

thermal-runaway lead time or field false alarms per operating hour.

HOW TO EXPLAIN THE RESULT
HAULSIM gives workload and energy. External accounting applies an energy
reserve. An assumed laboratory-cell model generates temperature. Forecasting
models are scored against generated temperature targets. These are initial
research stages, not a demonstrated thermal-runaway prediction system.
